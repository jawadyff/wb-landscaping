Our Story

A Family Business Built on Hard Work & Over 7 Years of Expertise

I still remember the first yard I ever transformed. I was just a teenager, watching a neglected, overgrown lot come to life under my hands — and feeling something click inside me. That was the moment I knew this was more than a job. It was a calling.

W&B Landscaping & Cleaning Service was born out of that same passion — a deep, genuine love for making outdoor spaces beautiful, functional, and something the whole family can be proud of. What started as a one-man operation with a beat-up truck and a determination to do every job right has grown into a trusted name across Poughkeepsie, Wappingers Falls, Beacon, and the entire Hudson Valley.

Over 7 years of hands-on experience have shaped who we are. We've survived brutal New York winters, demanding customers with high expectations (our favorite kind), and the kind of projects that test your grit and your skill in equal measure. Every single one made us better. Every yard we've maintained, every paver we've set, every driveway we've cleared of snow — they're all part of a story we're incredibly proud of.

We are a family-owned and operated company, and that means something. It means we show up. It means we care about the little details that a big corporate crew might skip. It means when you call us, you're talking to the people who will actually do the work — not a call center, not a middleman. Just us, committed to doing our absolute best on your property.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values & Commitment to the Hudson Valley

Every decision we make — from the materials we choose to the way we communicate — is guided by these principles.

01

Uncompromising Quality

We use only premium-grade materials — quality paving stones, professional-grade mulch, and robust hardscape systems. We never cut corners, because your outdoor space deserves results that last through New York's harsh winters and humid summers alike.

02

Radical Transparency

We believe in honest, upfront communication. Every estimate is detailed. If something changes mid-project, we tell you immediately. You'll never be surprised by a hidden cost or an unexplained charge on your invoice.

03

Respect for Your Property

Your home is your most important investment. We treat every job site with the utmost care — protecting existing plants and structures, cleaning up daily, and leaving your property better than we found it.

04

Community First

We are Poughkeepsie. We live here, raise our families here, and care deeply about the Hudson Valley communities we serve. Every project we complete is a reflection of our community pride.

05

Safety Above All

We are fully insured and our crews operate with the highest standards of safety on every job site. You are protected — and so are we — on every project, large or small.

06

Long-Term Relationships

Many of our clients hire us year after year — for seasonal cleanups, ongoing maintenance, and new projects. We value these long-term relationships enormously and work hard to earn them.

Why the Hudson Valley Trusts W&B Landscaping

In a region as diverse and beautiful as the Hudson Valley, your outdoor spaces deserve a contractor who truly understands the local landscape. The unique character of the Poughkeepsie area — from the rolling terrain of Dutchess County to the freeze-thaw cycles that test every hardscape installation — demands expertise that goes beyond aesthetics. Paver driveways in New York need proper base preparation to survive winter. Flower beds must be planted with the region's growing season in mind. We understand all of this because we've been working here for over seven years.

We are proud members of the Poughkeepsie community. Our team lives in the same neighborhoods we serve. When you hire W&B Landscaping, you are investing in a local business that reinvests directly into the community. We hire locally, source materials responsibly, and take genuine pride in the reputation we've built throughout Dutchess County and the Hudson Valley.
