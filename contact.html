Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you start my project?

Our lead time varies by season. During spring and summer (our busiest season), we typically book 2–4 weeks out. In fall and winter, we can often start within 1–2 weeks. Contact us early to secure your spot on our schedule.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes — always. We provide 100% free, no-obligation estimates for all projects. We visit your property, assess the site, and provide a clear quote with a full scope of work and pricing.

What areas do you service?

We serve Poughkeepsie, Wappingers Falls, Beacon, Hopewell Junction, Millbrook, Pleasant Valley, Hyde Park, Highland, Marlboro, Fishkill, and LaGrange. See our full service area page for details.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured and carry general liability insurance on every project. You can have complete peace of mind that your property is protected throughout the work.

Do you offer seasonal maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer recurring lawn care and maintenance packages, seasonal cleanup contracts, and snow removal contracts for the winter season. Contact us for details on available packages.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and major credit cards. We typically require a deposit at project start and final payment upon completion. All payment terms are clearly outlined before any work begins.
